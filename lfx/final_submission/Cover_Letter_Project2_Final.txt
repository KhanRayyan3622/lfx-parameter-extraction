How I Found Out:

Found this through the LFX portal. DFI at the hardware level is what I want to work on -

---

Why I Care:

Memory corruption is the #1 attack vector. Software defenses help but cost performance. 
Tightly-coupled DFI offers stronger guarantees with better performance.

Tight coupling is elegant - instead of complex coprocessors, integrate the mechanism 
directly into the pipeline. That's real architecture.
